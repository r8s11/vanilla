# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers and recruiters screening for IT Engineer, DevOps, or Cloud roles. They scan fast, look for trajectory and proof, and decide whether to download the resume or make contact. Fellow engineers are secondary readers, not the target.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and resume site for Roosevelt Segura, Cloud & DevOps-focused IT professional based in Brooklyn, NY with 10+ years supporting enterprise infrastructure across retail, healthcare, biotech, and scientific organizations. It must do two jobs equally: tell the career story (Tier 1/2 support, endpoint automation, and infrastructure work building toward a DevOps / Cloud role) and funnel toward the resume download or direct contact.

## Positioning

A hands-on infrastructure and service-management background a neighboring candidate cannot copy: 24/7 global support at 95%+ SLA, Microsoft 365 tenant ownership, ITSM built from zero, HIPAA-regulated environments, U.S. Army Reserve veteran, bilingual English/Spanish, AWS Cloud Practitioner and Azure AZ-900 certified.

## Operating Context

Evaluated in quick recruiter scans on desktop and phone. Success ends in one of two actions: downloading `resume-2026-10-devops-cloud.pdf` or opening the `mailto:mail@roosevelt.info` contact path. Static site deployed via Azure Static Web Apps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS; no framework, no build step.
- Redesign replaces the visual world only: all copy, facts, photos, and logos stay exactly as they are.
- Real assets in `assets/` (profile photo, company logos, cert badges, Army seal, rank insignia) must be reused, not replaced.
- No invented employers, credentials, metrics, or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Roosevelt Segura", tagline "IT Engineer // DevOps // Salsero". Existing asset set and resume PDF. No other binding visual constraint volunteered.

## Evidence on Hand

Full employment history with proof lines (Asset Inventories for NY Ross Buying Office, Peak Scientific, Foundation Medicine, Ventus, Jounce, Mass General), skills clusters, AWS/Azure badges, Army service record, SNHU education in progress, profile portrait — all in `index.html` and `assets/`. Source resume: `resume-2026-10-devops-cloud.pdf` (Asset Inventories has no logo asset; its station runs without a tile).

## Product Principles

1. Proof before adjectives: every claim rides with a number, scope, or outcome.
2. The scan must survive: role, trajectory, and contact reachable within seconds.
3. Trajectory over inventory: everything points toward DevOps / Cloud.
4. Nothing decorative at the expense of the funnel: story first, contact close.
5. Facts are fixed; only the telling changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA text pairs (incumbent pairs verified ≥ 4.5:1). Keyboard-operable job archive, skip link, reduced-motion support. Bilingual (English/Spanish) identity noted in content.
